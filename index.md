## 📖 How to Use GESTURE-FX

Using GESTURE-FX is as easy as 1-2-3:

### 1️⃣ Allow Camera Access
When the page opens, your browser will ask for permission to use your webcam. Click **"Allow"** or **"Yes"**. Without this, the app can't see you.

### 2️⃣ Make a Hand Gesture
Face your webcam and make one of these gestures:

- **Open Palm**: Show your whole hand, fingers together, palm facing the camera.
- **Peace Sign**: Make a "V" with your index and middle fingers.

### 3️⃣ Watch the Magic
When the app detects your gesture, the effect will appear **inside the frame** you created with your hands. Move around and see how the cartoon or glitch effect follows you!

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Tips

If something isn't working, try these fixes:

### ❌ Camera Not Working
- Make sure no other app (like Zoom or Teams) is using your camera
- Click the camera icon in your browser's address bar and select "Allow"
- Try restarting your browser

### ❌ Gestures Not Detected
- Make sure you're in a **well-lit room** — good lighting helps the camera see your hands
- Keep your hands **clearly visible** and not too close or too far from the camera
- Move slowly at first until the app "locks on" to your hand

### ❌ App Running Slowly
- Close other browser tabs and programs
- Try using Chrome instead of other browsers
- Make sure your computer isn't in power-saving mode
